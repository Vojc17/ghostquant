Content integration

TikTok publishing

GhostQuant supports an optional TikTok Content Posting API integration for authorized users who want to export eligible GhostQuant video content to their own TikTok account.

TikTok access is not required for the core market-analysis workflow. When the integration is used, account access is requested through TikTok's authorization process and is limited to the permissions granted by the user.

User control and transparency

Users remain in control of their TikTok account and authorization. GhostQuant does not request a TikTok password and does not claim ownership of a user's TikTok account or published content.

Details about information handling and service rules are available in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
