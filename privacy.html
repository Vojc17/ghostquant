Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how GhostQuant Gold handles information when a user connects a TikTok account.

Information processed

When authorized, GhostQuant may process information made available through TikTok APIs that is necessary for authentication and requested content-export functionality, including basic account identifiers and authorization credentials.

Use

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, maintain the requested connection, and perform user-requested content upload or posting functions. GhostQuant does not sell TikTok user data.

Retention and security

Authorization credentials are retained only as needed to provide the integration and are protected against unauthorized access.

Revocation and deletion

Users may revoke authorization through TikTok account settings. Users may also request deletion of information held by GhostQuant by contacting the service operator. Following a valid request, associated integration data will be deleted unless retention is required by law.

Third parties

TikTok's own terms and privacy practices apply to TikTok services.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the GhostQuant service operator using the public contact address supplied with the application.
