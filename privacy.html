Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GhostQuant Gold ("GhostQuant", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the GhostQuant service and its TikTok integration.

1. Information we process

When a user authorizes the TikTok integration, GhostQuant may process technical authorization information provided through TikTok's developer services, such as an access token, refresh token where applicable, authorization scopes, account/open identifier, token expiry information, publishing identifiers and API status/error responses required to operate and troubleshoot the integration.

GhostQuant does not request or store a user's TikTok password.

2. How we use information

We use integration information only to provide, maintain and troubleshoot the features the user authorized, including submitting eligible GhostQuant-generated content through TikTok's Content Posting API, checking publishing status, maintaining authorized access where permitted, and responding to support or deletion requests.

3. Market-analysis content

GhostQuant processes market and chart information used to generate XAUUSD analysis and visual content. This market information is not used to identify TikTok users.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to TikTok as necessary to use TikTok's developer services and may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only where required to operate the GhostQuant service. We do not use TikTok authorization data for unrelated advertising or data-broker purposes.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authorization credentials and integration data from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse. Sensitive credentials are intended to be kept out of publicly accessible source code and public web pages.

6. Retention

Integration information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the authorized functionality, maintain security and troubleshoot the service, or as required by law. Expired or no-longer-needed credentials and integration records may be deleted or replaced as part of normal operation.

7. User choices and revocation

Users may revoke GhostQuant's TikTok access through the applicable TikTok settings. Revocation may prevent future TikTok publishing until the user authorizes the integration again.

8. Data deletion requests

Users may request deletion of GhostQuant-held information associated with their TikTok integration by emailing ghostlevelfx@gmail.com. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant authorization without sending passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

9. Third-party services

The TikTok integration is also subject to TikTok's own terms, privacy practices and platform rules. GhostQuant does not control TikTok's independent processing of information.

10. Children

GhostQuant is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through its TikTok integration.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, integration or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, authorization questions or data deletion requests, contact ghostlevelfx@gmail.com.
